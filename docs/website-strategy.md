# Capital Generations Website Strategy

## Positioning

**Capital Generations provides managed outsourced B2B sales teams.**

The website deliberately moves the company beyond the narrow category of appointment setting. The core product is dedicated SDR and/or AE capacity supported by recruiting, training, sales management, client success, data operations, and reporting.

Primary promise:

> Build pipeline without building the entire team.

## Primary audience

The site speaks mainly to:

- Heads of Sales and commercial leaders
- growing B2B companies, often with fewer than 100 employees
- companies with a credible offer but insufficient sales capacity
- education, education technology, and education-adjacent companies
- teams that need pipeline without taking on another recruiting and management cycle

## Messaging hierarchy

1. Qualified pipeline is the business outcome.
2. The client receives a managed operating system, not just a rep.
3. Outreach is human-led, account-aware, and professionally delivered.
4. Capital Generations owns recruiting, training, coaching, accountability, and replacement responsibility.
5. Data and Power BI reporting make execution visible.
6. Education is a strong specialty without making the entire company education-only.

## Differentiation

The site positions Capital Generations as:

- dedicated rather than shared
- managed rather than merely staffed
- human-led rather than automated volume-first
- targeted rather than list-first
- transparent rather than activity-only
- professionally client-facing rather than a generic call-center model

The site does not use nationality, ethnicity, or geography as a negative competitive comparison.

## Conversion architecture

Primary conversion:

- Book a strategy conversation through Calendly

Secondary conversion:

- Submit a contact inquiry through Formspree

The site does not publish pricing. It is designed to earn a fit conversation rather than sell a fixed package online.

## Visual direction

- Capital Generations blue retained as the primary brand color
- deep navy, white, light blue, and restrained teal support colors
- modern B2B consultancy / revenue-operations feel
- no generic headset photography
- no client logos at launch
- no raw dashboard screenshots containing client information
- custom CSS and SVG-style diagrams explain the team and reporting model
- large, direct typography and generous spacing

## Proof strategy

The launch site uses anonymized prior outcomes:

- 600% increase in meetings held by month three
- 3× internal meeting output in a selected engagement
- 200+ meetings scheduled in H1 2023
- $3.5M pipeline generated in 2023
- 250% private-school growth during a seasonal ramp

Each result section states that outcomes vary and does not identify the client.
